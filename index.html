Company Overview

BJK Essentials (Pty) Ltd ("Always at Your Service") is a comprehensive South African security solutions provider and accredited training academy based in Bluegum View. Through its two core divisions, BJK Essentials serves both aspiring security professionals and clients needing reliable protection.

On-Demand Security & Guarding Services: We provide event protection, residential property guarding, armed response and other security services.

BJK Academy: Dedicated to industry excellence and skills development, BJK Academy provides accredited security training courses.

Vision

To deliver accessible, reliable, and tech-enabled security solutions while empowering the next generation of security professionals through accredited, high-standard training—ensuring peace of mind and safety for every client, always at your service.


Mission

To become South Africa’s leading integrated security provider and training institution, recognized for revolutionizing private security through digital innovation, operational excellence, and a highly skilled, vetted workforce.
